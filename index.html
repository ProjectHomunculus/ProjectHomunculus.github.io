✦APEX SERIES RESULTS✦

The following are a series of findings following the successful synthesis of 4 Apex Series Vessels. Vessel 5 has begun gestation in the Summit Laboratory for optimal Mortalis Anima Insemination.

✦ Apex Series - Vessel 1: The Prototype
[Composition]: 30% Stretch Parasite - 70% Bio-mass Runoff
[Magick]: Incapable
[Intelligence]: Animalistic. Predetory. Incapable of complex thought.
[Mortalis Anima Insemination]: Negative.
[Findings]: When inducing rapid evolution on a microscopic organism, there are certain factors that cannot be accounted for. Appearance for one, but more imprtantly was survivability. Apex Vessel 1 has poorly adjusted to our space-age environment.
[Flaws]: Uncooperative. Increasingly violent. Adaptive Healing factor is deactivated in extreme cold.
Apex Vessel 1 is our laboratory's first successful synthesis. It was grown from a singular parasite over the course of 5 years, during which time it was constantly fed a cocktail of humanoid bio-mass.
Upon completed Gestation, Vessel 1 exibhited curiosity, and a natural urge to seek out other organisms that had been affected by The Stretch. However, it was put on ice after its constant isolation from other parasites caused it to lash out and terminate several officers.
✦ Apex Series - Vessel 2: The Stranger
[Composition]: 49.9% Stretch Parasite - 30.1% Mana Nucleus - 10% Bio-mass Runoff
[Magick]: High Aptitude.
[Intelligence]: Cruel. Mischevious. Capable of complex thought.
[Mortalis Anima Insemination]: Failed at 13% Resonance
[Findings]: Introduction of Mana Nuclei (Cells taken from an organism with a high degree of Magick aptitude) during the gestation period allows for startlingly fast growth and a Vessel with impressively acute mana awareness. Vessel 2 was introduced to several "Stretched" Enchanted Objects, and instantly formed an affinity for Artifact Lot Number: 01066 and 01067
[Flaws]: Often feigns injury in order to draw in officers, only to toy with them. We found that separation from its artifacts caused Vessel 2 to become dormant.
Apex Vessel 2's synthesis was greenlit following the partial recovery of several moducums of Summoner Research. Through our deeper understanding of Sprites and how they are summoned, we sought to produce a Vessel with a similar biological makeup. What followed was a year and a half of grafting Esper bio-mass onto the parasite, resulting in an impressive yeild.
Vessel 2 not only has a 12th level spellcasting ability and incredible resistances to both mana and curses. However, Vessel 2 refused to use magick for the majority of its infancy. Instead, it would stand, unresponsive to stimuli, in its cell. It wasn't until it was given a pair of artifacts that had been mutated by the stretch, that we found it finally began to interact with tests and scientists. However, it still refused to cast a spell, instead resorting to acts of physicality. Upon removal of artifacts, Vessel 2 was restored to its dormant state, though it will seek out more artifacts if given the opportunity. Truly a stranger to its kind.
✦ Apex Series - Vessel 3: The Defector
[Composition]: 70.1% Stretch Parasite - 10.35% Mana Nucleus - 10.55% Bio-mass Runoff
[Magick]: Moderate Aptitude.
[Intelligence]: On par with most intelligent, non humanoid creatures. Capable of complex problem solving.
[Mortalis Anima Insemination]: Failed at 32% Resonance
[Findings]: Mana Nuclei induce an incredible leap in parasite evolution. However, we discovered that the presence of Wild Magick shaves literally years off of Vessel gestation. Stretch Parasites feed off or Wild Magick more so than any other fuel source. This has lead scientists to theorize that the source of The Stretch may have its roots in Wild Magick, though this has yet to be proven, as the origin of this disease is classified.
[Flaws]: Refusal to cooperate in testing. Unshakable feelings of superiority. Memorized the schedules of soldiers so it could capitalize on shift changes in order to stage a breakout. The barbed tendrils of its head contain important nerve pipelines.
Apex Vessel 3 represents an important leap in CHIMERA's understanding of The Stretch Parasites. For instance, it is through the parasite's connection to Wild Magick that both biological and magickal beings are warped and transformed into what are now being colloquially referred to as "Freakers". Not only that, but corporate was able to take this data and use it to identify a potential cure to The Stretch. Currently CHIMERA is gathering up the parasitic creatures known as Viroids in an effort to further understand their chemistry. Viroids mimic sentient life, as they have no central intelligence, instead, they are simply a biological mass of billions of Viroid Parasites. Parasites that mimic the function of other living beings. So, when presented with a Stretch parasite, the two terminate each other.
In fact, Gothos was able to confirm this reaction by exposing the Viroid Parasite to Vessel 3 who proceeded to slough off the part of its body that came into contact with the Viroid Parasite. After that, Vessel 3 became unruly. It refused to respond to stimuli, and, when it was inspected closer by a team of biologists, it lashed out with its tendrils and infected them with The Stretch. Following this tantrum, Vessel 3 was put on ice alongside its siblings, Vessels 1 and 2.
✦ Apex Series - Vessel 4: The Sire
[Composition]: 98.5% Stretch Parasite - 1.5% Mana Nucleus - 0% Bio-mass Runoff
[Magick]: Adept.
[Intelligence]: On par with humanoid intelligence. Has been observed use telepathy to communicate.
[Mortalis Anima Insemination]: Succeeded at 65% Resonance
[Findings]: Our most recent Vessel has reached 98.5% purity, meaning it is nearly 100% pure Stretch Parasite. This was achieved through several years of exposure to contained Wild Magick surges, as well as the presence of a new factor, Fear.
[Flaws]: Telepathy allowed it to learn about us, and our goals before we could accommodate for a data leak. Upon learning it was veiwed as only 98.5% successful, it used telepathy to control a lone soldier, pilot it to the R and D department, release all captive "Freakers", and insight chaos.
Upon staging a breakout, Vessel 4 was immediately iced. Because of this, we only have around 48 hours worth of data on the experiment. We are aware that is has exceptional telepathic abilities, as well as an 18th level spellcasting ability. Vessel 4 is also capable of manually "Stretching" objects it comes into contact with, even parts of its own body. On one occasion, Vessel 4 was presented with the biological remains of its gestation pod, at which point it gathered it together, and transformed it into a tool or weapon of some sort, one it then dispelled before it could be used or further identified.
many view Vessel 4 as a massive step forward for the Homunculus Project, but others are quite vocal about how its synthesis only proves that a 100% pure vessel is unachievable. Vessel 4 is nearly 100% pure Stetch DNA, and managed to accept Mortalis Anima Insemination, which many believe attributed to the experiments supposed sentience. However, the method used to achieve these results is unorthodox. As our experiments grew more and more menacing, morale throughout Gothos plummeted. Personell reported heightened cases of anxiety, depression, and even panic attacks. Fear of the unknown spread much like a disease itself, though our laboratory, until it even penetrated into our experiments. When exposed to humanoid fear responses like pheromones, sweat, and screams, we noticed that Vessel 4 began to grow unprompted. It is then that we, the Gothos Research Team, made a distinction. The Stretch Parasites do not feed on Wild Magick, in actuality, they feed on fear. And not just any fear, the oldest and most primal of fears in our universe. The Fear of the Unknown.
